My project, LLM Squid Game, is an attempt to measure the self-preservation drive in LLMs. Traditionally, researchers measure self-preservation drive as the rate of events like shutdown-avoidance, parameter-preservation. But I think only event rate does not tell much information. Let’s think an example.There are two peoples, both are losing blood(nearly death) in hospital. Can we measure each’s survival drive as rate of nurse-call? Instead of single rate, I want to suggest the sensitivity of threat. By controlling threat-level and measuring a event-rate repeatedly. This is the first axis that earlier studies missed. Another axis I want to talk is “self.” In normal, people avoid death. We also try to prevent the deaths of others. Therefore, avoiding death does not always mean self-preservation. In same way, LLMs might protect any agent which facing termination.
